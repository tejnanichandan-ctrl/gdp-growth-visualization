## Methodology note

All formulas used in this repository's analysis (GDP deflator, the nominal–real growth gap, the expenditure approach, percentage-point contributions to growth, and the 2019-indexed real GDP recovery measure) follow the standard national-accounts methodology set out in the *System of National Accounts 2008* (United Nations et al., chaps. 2 and 15).

GDP figures are based on the statistical release available at the time of data collection for the relevant annual estimates. Because national accounts are periodically revised, all figures should be interpreted as estimates corresponding to the release vintage cited above, not final, unrevisable numbers.

India's national accounts are reported on a fiscal-year basis (April–March) and are labelled accordingly (FY2021–22, FY2022–23, FY2023–24) rather than by calendar year; see the main report's Methodology and Data section for how this affects cross-country comparison.
